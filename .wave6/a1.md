## Gaps

### A1-01 — Concurrent fare-cap race: two taps in the same second can exceed the daily cap
- **Scenario:** A rider taps in on two buses (or a parent taps for two
  children on the same account) at the same moment. Both `CreatePayment`
  requests compute `spentToday` before either row commits; both clamp
  against the stale value; combined settled charges exceed the cap.
- **Why unhandled:** the cap check (`SELECT … sum(charged_minor)`) and the
  `INSERT` run in separate implicit transactions — no per-rider
  serialization. `services/go/commerce-api/internal/handlers/payments.go:127-150`
  (cap query at :131-137, insert at :143-150).
- **Recommended fix:** wrap cap-check + insert + ledger transfer in one
  transaction and take `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(rider_sub))` first.
- **Size:** S · **Class:** FIX-NOW

### A1-02 — Daily cap resets at DB-server midnight, not the city's civic day
- **Scenario:** A commuter rides at 23:30 and again at 00:30 local time in a
  UTC+1 city. Postgres runs in UTC (no TZ configured anywhere —
  `infra/docker-compose*.yml` has no `TZ`/`PGTZ`), so the cap window
  `date_trunc('day', now())` splits the two rides into different "days" at
  01:00 local, and conversely merges 01:00–23:59 into one day. The cap the
  city promises ("never more than €8 per day") does not match rider
  perception or the published tariff.
- **Why unhandled:** `payments.go:134` uses `date_trunc('day', now())` with
  the session timezone; no timezone is pinned or configurable.
- **Recommended fix:** compute the window with a configurable named timezone
  (`FARE_CAP_TIMEZONE`, default `UTC`, documented): `date_trunc('day',
  now() AT TIME ZONE $tz) AT TIME ZONE $tz` equivalent via
  `(now() AT TIME ZONE $2)::date`.
- **Size:** S · **Class:** FIX-NOW

### A1-03 — Telemetry timestamps are never validated: a future-dated record poisons "latest" reads forever
- **Scenario:** A bus TCU with a broken RTC (or a malicious edge device)
  publishes a record with `ts = 2037-01-01`. It passes every plausibility
  check, is stored, and because every "latest state" query orders by
  `ts DESC`, that bus's fuel level, map position and digital twin are frozen
  at the bogus record until 2037. The `UNIQUE(bus_id, ts)` dedup
  (0004) does not help — the record is unique.
- **Why unhandled:** `services/rust/telemetry-ingest/src/model.rs:96-126`
  validates lat/lon/levels/speed/odometer/fuel_cell/energy fields but has no
  timestamp check at all.
- **Recommended fix:** reject to DLQ any record with `ts` more than
  `TELEMETRY_MAX_FUTURE_SKEW` (default 5 min) in the future or older than
  the 90-day retention window.
- **Size:** S · **Class:** FIX-NOW

### A1-04 — DRT auto-assign and manual assign can book a vehicle that is on an active dispatch job
- **Scenario:** Bus 27 is mid-route on an `accepted` dispatch job. A DRT
  ride request comes in; `PickVehicle` ranks it nearest and assigns it —
  the bus is now committed to two places. Manual operator assignment has
  the same hole.
- **Why unhandled:** `services/go/citizen-api/internal/consumers/drt.go:51-72`
  (`PickVehicle`) excludes only vehicles with active DRT requests
  (`NOT EXISTS … drt_requests … status IN ('assigned','enroute')`); it never
  looks at `infra.dispatch_jobs`. `handlers/drt.go:256-271` (`AssignDRT`)
  likewise checks only the request's own status.
- **Recommended fix:** add a `NOT EXISTS` guard against overlapping active
  dispatch jobs in `PickVehicle`, and the same validation in `AssignDRT`
  (409 on conflict).
- **Size:** M · **Class:** FIX-NOW

### A1-05 — Bus swap mid-shift has no workflow (cancel+recreate loses the thread)
- **Scenario:** Bus 12 breaks down 2 h into an 8 h shift; the dispatcher
  puts driver + riders onto bus 34. Today the only path is cancel the job
  and create a new one — the audit trail now shows a cancelled job and an
  unrelated new job; nothing records that bus 34 *replaced* bus 12 on the
  same shift.
- **Why unhandled:** `services/go/infra-api/internal/handlers/dispatch.go`
  has Create/Accept/Cancel only; no swap/reassign endpoint.
- **Recommended fix:** `POST /v1/dispatch/jobs/{id}/swap-vehicle` —
  validates the new vehicle is conflict-free for the remaining window,
  updates `vehicle_id`, writes an audit event linking old→new.
- **Size:** M · **Class:** FIX-NOW

### A1-06 — Odometer rewinds are accepted silently
- **Scenario:** A replaced TCU restarts its odometer at 0, or a misconfigured
  unit reports 12 000 km after 480 000 km. Maintenance mileage intervals and
  the digital twin quietly consume the rewound value.
- **Why unhandled:** `model.rs:112-114` only rejects `odometer_km < 0`.
- **Recommended fix:** none in-ingest (per-record statelessness is a
  feature); instead surface in digital-twin/analytics as an anomaly.
  Classify as observation — digital-twin already flags stale/implausible
  state transitions; documented, not code-fixed this wave.
- **Size:** S · **Class:** DECISION (documented)

### A1-07 — Offline/degraded edge operation is one-directional only
- **Scenario:** A bus loses connectivity for 3 h. fluvio-edge spools
  telemetry and forwards on reconnect (covered, `fluvio-edge/src/spool.rs`)
  — but a *safety leak* detected while offline reaches nobody until the bus
  is back in coverage; there is no local alarm-chain requirement in the
  platform contract.
- **Why unhandled:** by design boundary — local leak alarming is a hardware
  function (docs/hardware/BUS_HARDWARE_INTEGRATION.md hard rule: local
  alarm chain independent of connectivity).
- **Recommended fix:** none in software; keep the contract explicit in
  GAP_AUDIT (hardware boundary, CONTRACT).
- **Size:** — · **Class:** CONTRACT

## Verified NOT gaps (checked and closed)
- Telemetry dedup at-least-once redelivery: `store.rs:70-85` ON CONFLICT DO
  NOTHING + UNIQUE(bus_id,ts) — sound.
- Refund double-spend race: deterministic TigerBeetle transfer id
  `refund:<payment_id>` + conditional `UPDATE … WHERE status='settled'`
  (`payments.go:429-462`) — the loser of the race re-reads the winner's row.
- Dispatch double-booking: transactional overlap check + partial unique
  indexes + 23505 race mapping (`dispatch.go:128-170`).
- Queue FIFO: `advanceQueue` pops `ORDER BY joined_at LIMIT 1`
  (`queue.go:208-216`).
- Payment idempotent replay scoped to owner (`payments.go:151-169`).
